    Parts & Controls

    1. Shuffle/Playlists

      Press to start shuffle playback. Press and hold to start playing a playlist.

    2. Volume +/-

      Press to adjust the volume.

    3. On/Off

      Press and hold the On/Off button for about 3 seconds.

    4. Operating Lamp

      The operating lamp indicates the remaining battery power. The lamp will blink in green at 5 second intervals during playback.

    5. USB

      Attach your Walkman® to the USB cradle (supplied). Connect the USB plug to charge the battery and/or add songs from your PC.

    6. Play/Pause

      Press to start or pause playback. Press and hold to start ZAPPIN™ playback.

    7. Back/Forward

      Press to skip to the previous or next song. Press and hold to skip to the beginning of the previous or next folder.

    8. Reset

      Press the 'Reset' button with a small pin to reset your Walkman®

    9. Power Lock Switch

      Slide the Power Lock Switch to lock or unlock the Power button.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long can the W Series Sports Walkman® stay submerged?

    The W Series Sports Walkman® can stay continuously submerged at a depth of up to 2m (6.6 feet).

    Will the W Series Sports Walkman® work in salt water?

    No, the W Series Sports Walkman® is fully functional in freshwater, but can be damaged if submerged in salt water.

    How much music does this hold?

    Approximately 1,000 songs.(128kbps). 1 song = approximately 4 minutes.
